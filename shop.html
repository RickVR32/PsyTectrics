Good to know

When will my order ship?

These are pre-orders. Your game ships as soon as the first print run lands, and we'll email you the moment it's on its way.

Do I pay now?

Yes. You pay at checkout so your deck is reserved in the first run. Payments are handled securely by Stripe, and you can use card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Klarna where available.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time before it ships for a full refund: just reply to your receipt or contact us. After delivery you still have 14 days to change your mind, as long as the deck is unused and in its wrapping.

How much is delivery?

UK delivery is £3.95, or free when you order two or more decks. We only ship within the UK for now.

Is the Narcissist Card Game about diagnosing people?

No. It's a self-reflection game, not a diagnosis. It's about patterns everyone falls into sometimes, and it's built so nobody is singled out.

Is Bad Habits! OK for younger children?

It's designed for ages 7 to 12. Younger children can join in with a grown-up reading the cards.

Can I buy for a school, clinic or shop?

Yes. For 10 or more decks, or wholesale, get in touch and we'll send a quote.
